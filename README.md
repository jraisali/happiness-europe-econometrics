# Leisure Spending & Happiness in Europe

Econometrics project studying the relationship between **household spending on leisure and culture and happiness across European Union countries**.

## Research question

Do higher levels of household spending on leisure and culture correspond to higher levels of happiness?

## Data

The analysis covers the **27 countries of the European Union**.

Main data sources:

- INSEE
- World Happiness Report

Variables studied include:

- Happiness score
- Household expenditure on leisure and culture
- GDP per capita
- Inflation
- Geographic region within Europe

## Methodology

The project uses several econometric models:

- Simple linear regression
- Multiple regression
- Model comparison
- Breusch-Pagan tests for heteroskedasticity
- Statistical significance analysis

The objective was to understand how leisure spending relates to happiness while controlling for other economic variables.

## Main findings

The analysis found a positive and statistically significant relationship between leisure and cultural spending and happiness.

GDP per capita was also strongly associated with happiness, while inflation was not statistically significant in the tested model.

The study also identified differences between Eastern and Western European countries.
